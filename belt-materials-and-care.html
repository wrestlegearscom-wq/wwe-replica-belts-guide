Wrestling Belt Materials, Display and Care

Learn what to check in belt materials and how to keep a collectible looking its best.

Home / Wrestling Belt Materials, Display and Care

Understand the listed materials

Replica and commemorative belts can use different plate and strap materials. Product descriptions should explain the materials used. If a listing does not specify them, contact the seller rather than guessing from photographs.

Keep the belt clean

Use a soft, clean cloth and follow any care instructions supplied with the product. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive pads and untested cleaners, which may damage finishes or decorative surfaces.

Choose a suitable display location

Keep a belt away from excessive moisture, direct sunlight and heat sources. Use a stable display stand or wall mount that is appropriate for the belt's dimensions and weight.

Store it safely

If you are storing a belt, keep it in a clean, dry place and avoid placing heavy objects on top of it. Protect decorative surfaces from scratches and unnecessary contact.

When comparing products, review the construction information and photos in the WrestleGears WWE belts collection.
